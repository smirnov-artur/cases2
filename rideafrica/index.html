The idea

Ride Africa was conceived like so many ideas: around a bar with friends.

Cheap bikes, endless roads, almost no riders.

Rex was bewildered at the lack of motorcycle culture on his travels through Africa. Motorcycles are cheap to buy, easy to maintain and an efficient way to get around, and the riding, on road and off, is second to none: the dunes of Morocco, the jungles, mountains and game reserves of Tanzania, the red desert of Namibia, the sparsely populated corners of South Africa.

He has applied his experience from designing adventures for Himalayan Heroes to the colourful continent, to make it far more accessible to all motorcyclists.

Challenging ridingRoad and off-road, dunes to mountain passes
